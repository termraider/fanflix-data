# codewatch-feed Privacy Policy

_Effective: November 25, 2026_

codewatch-feed is a small, read-only Reddit app. It is installed on its own private subreddit and reads public posts from a public Reddit community about buying and selling digital movie codes, to keep a private record of listed titles and prices for personal price research.

## What it reads
- Public posts in that community: title, text, flair, author username, time, link, and whether a post was edited or removed.
- Public comments on recent posts: text, author username, time, and which comment they reply to.

It does not read private messages, user profiles, emails, or anything that isn't already public on Reddit.

## What it stores
- A copy of the public posts and comments above, saved to a private GitHub repository that only its operator can access.
- In the app's own Devvit storage: post IDs, a short fingerprint of each post's text (to notice edits), and comment counts. These expire after 45 days.

It does not collect emails, real names, passwords, payment details, location, or private messages.

## Sharing
The data is not sold, and it is not posted or published on Reddit. The only outside service the app sends data to is GitHub (api.github.com), to save the copy in the private repository.

## Your choices
To ask for your posts or comments to be removed from the record, message the moderators of r/codewatch_feed_dev: https://www.reddit.com/message/compose?to=r/codewatch_feed_dev

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
